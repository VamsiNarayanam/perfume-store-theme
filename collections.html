Every mood Has a Fragrance

Four families, each built around the way you want to feel. Choose one, and you will know it when a scent stops you.

Shop collections

Floral Reverie

A garden at the hour before rain, when every petal holds its breath. Floral Reverie is made of blooms that feel close and unhurried, soft against the skin and never sweet.

Flowers, but only the quiet ones.

Woody & Earthy

Warm shelter. Dry wood, and the scent of a drawer after rain. It settles close, then stays — unhurried, certain, the one you reach for when the evening goes quiet.

Rooted, unhurried and quietly confident.
Explore collections

Fresh & Aquatic

Salt water on warm skin, the first minutes of light. A pale citrus over the harbour. Bright as opening a window. It clears the air, then stays, as if the tide had left water on your skin.

The feeling of a clear horizon.
