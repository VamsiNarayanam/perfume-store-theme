Before you write

Do I need an appointment?

Weekdays are open for a short sitting. Saturday afternoons fill, so a note ahead keeps a chair for you.

Can I smell before I buy?

Yes. Blotters are on the table, and skin tests are welcome. We would rather you leave with the right bottle than a quick one.

Do you ship the same formulas?

The bottles that leave the studio are the ones on the shelf. 30, 50, and 100 ml, packed the day they are reserved.
